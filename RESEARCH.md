## What people ask (social)
Reddit itself was blocked to research tools. Evidence comes from r/FIREPakistan metadata (~80k members), Zameen/PakWheels forums and news. **Verify by hand on Reddit before relying on this.**
- "I have 5–10 lakh / 20–25 lakh / 50 lakh–1 crore, where?" "Need Rs X per month for retired parents." "Halal only." "Overseas: plot or keep dollars?" "Which broker app / how to become a filer?"
- Consensus: short term → MMF / T-bills; retirees → Behbood; long term → index / equity funds; halal → Islamic MMF, Sarwa, Meezan funds; 10–25% gold hedge; become a filer.
- Warnings: plot files beyond approved land (42,000 files vs 6,000 plots, ICHS 2026), "guaranteed monthly profit" mudaraba Ponzis, crypto/forex signal groups, BC committee organisers who disappear.
- Disagreements: plot vs PSX, whether NSS/Islamic banking is riba, gold vs USD.
- Pain points: tax and filer confusion, opening accounts, rates changing often, conflicting advice, Urdu.

## Regulation (important for the app)
- Personalised securities advice needs a **SECP licence** (Securities & Futures Advisers Regulations 2017; Digital Investment Advisory licence exists). Selling funds needs distributor registration.
- MVP positioning: **educational comparison** (rates, after-tax and after-inflation maths, generic model mixes, crowd posts clearly labelled as opinion), no "buy this specific fund" calls, standard disclaimer. For real advice: partner with a licensed adviser/AMC or apply to SECP's sandbox.

## Gap
No neutral tool compares CDNS + InvestPak + MMFs + banks + PSX + gold + REITs, after tax (filer vs non-filer), against inflation, with halal and eligibility filters, in Urdu, plus crowd advice. Existing apps (Mahaana, Al Meezan, NBP Funds, Sarmaaya, KTrade, InvestPak, CDNS) are each tied to one product or AMC.
